02 / Planned rewards

Your HUSH.
Your briefcase.

We’re exploring activity-funded holder rewards in a third-party bridged Zcash asset on Solana. The final fee rate and distribution provider are still being evaluated.

01 — HOLD

Hold HUSH

Keep HUSH in a compatible Solana wallet. Final eligibility requirements will be published before launch.

02 — COLLECT

Activity funds the pool

The proposed model collects token fees and converts a designated holder allocation into the selected Solana ZEC asset. Fee collection alone does not convert or distribute rewards.

03 — RECEIVE

Automatic payouts, planned

The goal is automatic distributions based on eligible HUSH balances. The provider, eligibility rules and payout schedule are not finalized.

Selected research asset: Zcash (OmniBridge), a third-party bridged representation on Solana. This is not native ZEC, and Solana payouts are not automatically shielded Zcash transactions. Final configuration, payout timing, eligibility and deductions will be disclosed before launch. No fixed payout is promised.

03 / The Zcash commitment

A quiet commitment
to Zcash.

2.5% of HUSH supply. Permanently held by commitment to Zcash.

PLANNED AT LAUNCH

HUSH Zcash Donation Reserve

The creator plans to purchase 5% of total supply at launch, keep 2.5% personally, and immediately transfer the other 2.5% into a separate, publicly monitored Solana wallet.

The reserve’s HUSH will never be sold or used for personal purposes. All rewards it actually receives are dedicated to Zcash Foundation donations, after necessary, disclosed conversion and transfer fees.
